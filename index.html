01 About

A boutique address
built for the way you travel.

Sunstone Suites sits in the heart of Ratnagiri. Close enough to the highway for an easy arrival, close enough to the sea for a sunset drive. The lobby is quiet, the hallways warmly lit, the rooms generous.

A property by Jagrut Retreats, built on a simple idea: business-class comfort, hospitality that remembers your name, and nothing you don't need.
